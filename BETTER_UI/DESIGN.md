## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an airy, approachable, and refined aesthetic. It marries quiet minimalism with organic softness, cultivating an atmosphere that feels simultaneously modern, serene, and deeply welcoming. The experience speaks to users who appreciate clarity without sterility, and warmth without clutter.

### Aesthetic Attributes
- **Refined Softness:** Gentle visual contours, generous negative space, and pill-shaped interactive anchors soften cognitive load and invite interaction.
- **Understated Elegance:** Rather than competing for attention with loud graphics or aggressive gradients, the interface leans on subtle tonal shifts, crisp type contrast, and purposeful spacing.
- **Harmonious Calm:** Surfaces feel warm and paper-like rather than stark cold white, grounding the user in an environment designed for prolonged, stress-free engagement.

## Colors

The palette grounds itself in muted natural sophistication. Light surfaces are infused with a delicate warm tint rather than harsh clinical white, while typography balances crisp contrast without stark black harshness.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#355E53` - Deep Sage):** An understated, earthy botanical shade providing confident yet tranquil hierarchy across primary actions, active tabs, and key navigational landmarks.
- **Secondary (`#5C768D` - Slate Indigo):** A soft, cool counterweight used for secondary actions, informative tags, and stabilizing visual chrome.
- **Tertiary (`#C48B71` - Soft Terracotta):** A warm, restrained accent used sparingly for notifications, highlights, and human touchpoints.
- **Neutral Primary (`#1E252B` - Deep Charcoal):** Provides high-legibility readability for headlines and primary text, eliminating eye fatigue caused by pure `#000000`.
- **Surfaces & Backgrounds:** Base canvas sits at a featherweight warm ivory (`#FAF9F5`), stepping cleanly to pure white (`#FFFFFF`) for elevated cards and surfaces, subtly bounded by soft slate hairpins (`#E8ECE9`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy utilizes Plus Jakarta Sans throughout. Its gentle geometric curves, open apertures, and subtle contemporary humanist undertones offer unmatched legibility and an intrinsically friendly, approachable cadence.

### Formatting Principles
- **Headlines:** Display and headline levels leverage medium and semi-bold weights with slight negative letter tracking, creating a cohesive, editorial character.
- **Body:** Standard body copy prioritizes generous line-height ratios (1.5–1.6) to guarantee effortless readability across expansive layouts.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Micro labels maintain slight positive tracking with elevated medium-to-semibold weights to ensure sharp definition at reduced scale.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid system pinned to a maximum container width of `75rem` (1200px), maintaining centered focus and comfortable horizontal scanning margins on ultra-wide screens.

### Rhythm & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Vertical stack density remains relaxed with `1.5rem` section spacing.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout with `2rem` outer padding and `1.5rem` interior column gutters.
- **Pacing Philosophy:** Abundant vertical breathing room between functional modules prevents density fatigue and elevates the sense of calm.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids harsh, dark drop shadows. Depth is communicated predominantly through subtle tonal layering and extra-diffused, ambient-tinted shadows that mimic gentle ambient daylight.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#FAF9F5` base background; zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer):** `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by an ultra-soft ambient shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(30, 37, 43, 0.04), 0 1px 3px rgba(30, 37, 43, 0.02)` and a hairline border of `rgba(30, 37, 43, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Popovers & Dropdowns):** `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(30, 37, 43, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(30, 37, 43, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** `0 20px 48px -12px rgba(30, 37, 43, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(30, 37, 43, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The shape language centers on gentle, organic curvature, communicating comfort and tactile invitation at every interaction level.

### Geometric Rules
- **Interactive Elements:** Buttons, search inputs, tabs, and tags default to full pill radius (`9999px`), emphasizing tactile friendliness and clickability.
- **Containers & Cards:** Card modules and content panels adopt large rounded corners (`rounded-2xl`, 1.5rem to 2rem), balancing structure with softness.
- **Inner Framing:** Nested elements follow concentric geometry—internal media or sub-containers reflect proportional, slightly tighter corner radii (typically `1rem` to `1.25rem`) to maintain balanced margins.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full pill radius, Deep Sage (`#355E53`) background, crisp white text. Hover state gently shifts to `#2B4C43` with a soft elevation bloom.
- **Secondary:** Transparent fill, hairline border (`#D2DBD5`), Charcoal (`#1E252B`) text. Hover introduces a subtle sage-tinted wash (`#F2F6F4`).
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No border, muted text (`#5C768D`), pill hover background (`#EFF3F1`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Pill Badges:** Complete pill form with vertical padding of `0.25rem` and horizontal padding of `0.75rem`.
- **States:** Muted Sage (Sage fill at 10% opacity with solid Sage text) for confirmed/active states; Warm Terracotta tint for highlights; Soft Slate for neutral tags.

### Input Fields
- **Container:** Generous `0.75rem` vertical and `1.25rem` horizontal padding with full pill radius or smooth `rounded-xl` depending on contextual scale.
- **Surface:** `#FFFFFF` fill with a crisp, low-contrast border (`#DEE4E0`). On focus: soft primary sage ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(53, 94, 83, 0.15)`) with a `#355E53` border.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkboxes:** Rounded squares (`0.375rem` radius). Unselected state features a subtle `#CBD5D0` border; selected state fills with `#355E53` featuring a pure white check icon.
- **Radios:** Concentric circle with pill-smooth transitions and primary Sage fill on selection.

### Cards
- **Base Style:** `#FFFFFF` background, `rounded-2xl` corners (1.5rem), faint `1px` border in `rgba(30, 37, 43, 0.06)`, and Level 1 ambient drop shadow.
- **Card Padding:** Minimum of `1.5rem` internal clearance, ensuring breathing room around headers, illustrations, and actions.

### Avatars & Media Containers
- **Avatars:** Circular or smooth squircle (`rounded-2xl`) framing with a clean `2px` white border when overlapping.
- **Media Framing:** Imagery and illustrations sit comfortably inside `rounded-xl` frames with subtle neutral boundary lines.
